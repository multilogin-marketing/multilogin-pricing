## How Multilogin Pricing Works

Every Multilogin plan bundles cloud phones, browser profiles, and residential proxies into one dashboard, on one subscription. Users don't pay separately for each tool.

Key pricing principles:

- **One platform, both environments.** Android cloud phones and browser profiles are available together on every plan, including the Free plan.
- **Built-in proxies.** Residential proxy traffic is included in every plan and matched to each profile's location.
- **Mobile minutes included.** Every plan comes with mobile minutes for cloud phone usage. Extra minutes are billed pay-as-you-go.
- **Rollover.** Unused proxy traffic and mobile minutes roll over as long as the subscription stays active. (On the Free plan, the 30 minutes and 200 MB are one-time allocations with no top-up.)
- **Annual discount.** Paying annually costs meaningfully less per month than paying monthly.
- **Built for people and AI agents.** Every plan includes API access, plus ADB and support for Selenium, Puppeteer, Playwright, and Postman for automated workflows.

## Add-ons and Pay-as-you-go Rates

| Add-on | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Proxy traffic | **$3.50 per GB** | Residential proxies from a 30M+ IP pool. Unused traffic rolls over on paid plans. |
| Mobile minutes | **From $0.0073/min (larger packages) to $0.009/min (smaller packages)** | Pay only for time used. Larger packages cost less per minute. Unused minutes roll over on paid plans. |

Top-ups for minutes and proxy traffic are available on paid plans only. On the Free plan, both allocations are one-time and don't renew.

## Multilogin Free Plan

Multilogin's Free plan has **no time limit and requires no credit card**. It's a real starting point, not a trial.

- **Price:** $0, no time limit
- **Profiles:** up to 5 total (browser + mobile combined), with a maximum of 1 mobile profile (cloud phone); the rest can be browser profiles
- **Mobile minutes:** 30, one-time allocation (no top-up on Free)
- **Proxy traffic:** 200 MB, one-time allocation (no top-up on Free)
- **Storage:** cloud storage only (local storage isn't available on Free)
- **Inactivity rule:** if an account is inactive for 7 days, all profiles on it are deleted automatically

Upgrade to a paid plan when you need more profiles, minutes, or proxy traffic.
